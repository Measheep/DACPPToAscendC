# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project spaces must remain respectful, technically constructive, and safe for
participants of every background and level of experience. Examples of welcome
behavior include clear feedback, good-faith collaboration, crediting others'
work, accepting correction, and focusing disagreement on evidence and code.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project
events, and private communication undertaken in an official project role.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a verified repository maintainer. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers may
remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or impose
a permanent ban according to the seriousness and recurrence of the behavior.

Maintainers must handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and document enforcement decisions for the responsible maintainer group.
